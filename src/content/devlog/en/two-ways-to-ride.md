## A day with a story

GDD v1.2 describes Story Mode as a connected experience of delivery, daily life, economic pressure, debt management, characters, and narrative progression. The central journey is to get by, pay off debt, and regain freedom.

The city is planned around Ingye-dong in Suwon, using OpenStreetMap (OSM) data with creator curation to support play and narrative.

## Time focused on deliveries

Free Delivery Mode aims to generate an OSM-based map from a real-world area selected by the player. It focuses on delivery simulation without a mandatory dependency on the story, life, or debt systems.

The two modes share the act of delivering, but give it different contexts. We want that distinction to remain clear in how we introduce the game.

## Design and current status

These are **design goals**. This article does not mean that map generation, life systems, or either mode are complete. Verified development updates will describe what is actually playable.
