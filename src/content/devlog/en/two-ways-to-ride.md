If you only want to make a delivery or two, sitting through the debt story first sounds like a chore. The current plan splits the game into two modes.

Story mode uses OpenStreetMap data from around Ingye-dong in Suwon, with the neighborhood adjusted for the story. Delivery earnings go toward bills and debt.

For free delivery, the plan is to pick a real area and generate a map for it. You can take orders without having to advance the story or pay off debt. Both modes and map generation are still plans.
