## A starting point in the everyday

Mechureal is an independent game development team. Our first project explores a day of deliveries in a city. We see possibilities for play in seemingly small actions: accepting an order, finding an address, or counting the day's earnings.

**BROKE RIDER SIMULATOR** is in development as a first-person, single-player delivery, life, and comedy simulation. We use Unity 6.3 and target Windows and macOS.

## What we will share here

Our official devlog will cover design intentions, development decisions, and verified changes. We will distinguish plans from implemented features.

A release date and playable build have not been announced. Follow this site or the language-specific RSS feed for future news.

> This is an introduction to the team and the project's direction, not an announcement of completed features.
